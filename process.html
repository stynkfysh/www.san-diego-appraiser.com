How it works

From First Call to Finished Report

Six steps, the same for a Coronado condo or a Julian cabin.

  1. 1

    Tell me what the appraisal is for

    Intended use drives everything else: the effective date, the type of report and the level of detail. A two-minute conversation settles the scope.

  2. 2

    Receive a quote and a date

    You receive the fee, the report type and a delivery estimate before anything is scheduled. Nothing proceeds until you approve it.

  3. 3

    Inspection or records review

    For standard reports, the home is measured, photographed and observed inside and out. Desktop work begins immediately from records and listing data.

  4. 4

    Market research and analysis

    Comparable sales are selected from the same market segment and adjusted for differences in size, condition, location, view and date of sale.

  5. 5

    Report delivery

    A signed PDF report arrives by email, ready to forward to an attorney, CPA, trustee or court.

  6. 6

    Questions afterward

    Follow-up questions about the report are welcome. Testimony or a value update for a later date can be arranged if the matter calls for it.
