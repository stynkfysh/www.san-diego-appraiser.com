Mid-City Mesas & Canyons

Postwar mesa-top tracts and canyon-rim streets in the middle of the city, dominated by single-story homes from the 1950s through the 1970s.

Clairemont92110, 92111, 92117Clairemont is a large postwar mesa community north of Mission Bay, divided by canyons and cut through by Clairemont Drive and Balboa Avenue.Serra Mesa92123, 92111Serra Mesa is a central San Diego mesa top that sits between Mission Valley, Kearny Mesa, and Murphy Canyon.Tierrasanta92124With Mission Trails Regional Park on one edge and Murphy Canyon on another, Tierrasanta occupies a self-contained mesa whose 1970s master plan set aside generous open space and favored quiet cul-de-sacs.Allied Gardens92120Allied Gardens is a postwar neighborhood in the Navajo community along the north side of Mission Gorge Road and the San Diego River.San Carlos92119San Carlos sits at the eastern edge of the city of San Diego, with Lake Murray and Cowles Mountain close to its boundaries.Del Cerro92120Del Cerro is a hillside community on the northern side of Interstate 8 east of the College Area, known for winding streets, larger lots, and long views over the surrounding valleys.University City92122University City stretches between Interstate 5 and Interstate 805 beside the UC San Diego campus and the Golden Triangle employment district.Mira Mesa92126Mira Mesa is a large inland community on the mesa north of Miramar, planned in stages from the late 1960s onward and tied to the nearby Sorrento Mesa and Miramar employment centers.
