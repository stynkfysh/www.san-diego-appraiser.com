Home Appraisals in Crest

Crest is a hillside community above El Cajon, between Dehesa and Harbison Canyon, with rural lots and broad views. Appraisals here are driven by terrain and access.

The Crest Housing Market

Homes are mainly detached custom and ranch houses on lots that range from one to several acres, built across several decades. Some properties include horse facilities, guest houses and workshops. Condos are essentially absent, and HOAs are scarce. Pricing lies near the middle of the county, with higher figures for larger parcels and views.

What Shapes Value in Crest

Septic systems, private wells or water district service, road conditions and the fire hazard setting are leading factors. Steep sites affect usable area and building cost, and unpermitted additions are common. Comparable data may be scattered, so adjustments for land size and view need careful support.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Landmarks include Crest Ridge, Dehesa Road, the eastern edge of Granite Hills and the hills along Harbison Canyon Road.

Our Crest property has a septic system; will the appraiser need documents about it?

Septic records, pumping and any inspections are helpful, and the appraiser notes the condition and capacity. Problems or limited capacity can affect value and lender conditions. County records supplement what the owner provides.
