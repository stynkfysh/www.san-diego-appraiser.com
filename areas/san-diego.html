Central City of San Diego · Urban

Home Appraisals in City of San Diego

The urban core of the city spans the downtown grid, the bayfront, and the older neighborhoods that ring Balboa Park. For an appraiser, it is a collection of very different submarkets that happen to share a mailing address and a municipal government.

The City of San Diego Housing Market

Downtown and the Marina District are dominated by high-rise condominiums and loft conversions, while Little Italy mixes newer mid-rise buildings with a few older cottages. Bankers Hill, Golden Hill, Sherman Heights and South Park hold Craftsman bungalows, Spanish Revival duplexes and Victorian-era homes on small, narrow lots. Many properties are attached or share walls, and HOA dues, special assessments and reserve funding are routine parts of the analysis. Mello-Roos is uncommon in the older areas but appears in some newer redevelopment projects. Pricing sits across a wide range, from entry-level condos to restored historic houses that rank well above the county norm.

What Shapes Value in City of San Diego

Condominium appraisals depend heavily on the building: HOA financial health, owner-occupancy ratios, pending litigation and unit floor level can matter more than the street address. Historic designations, Mills Act contracts and the proximity of freeway, trolley and bay views all call for careful comparable selection.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Reference points include Balboa Park, Petco Park, the Embarcadero waterfront, Little Italy's India Street corridor and the Gaslamp Quarter.

My father owned a downtown condo and a duplex in Golden Hill; can both be valued as of his date of death?

Yes. Each property is appraised separately, with an effective date set to the date of death so that the stepped-up basis is supported. The condo is analyzed with sales from the same building or comparable towers, while the duplex relies on nearby multi-unit or two-unit sales plus rental data.
