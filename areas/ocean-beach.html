Home Appraisals in Ocean Beach

Ocean Beach lies on the west end of the Point Loma peninsula, with Newport Avenue as its main street and the pier and dog beach as its edges. Appraisers see a market of older cottages and small homes that has remained notably informal.

The Ocean Beach Housing Market

Housing is chiefly modest beach cottages, bungalows and duplexes from the 1920s through the 1950s, along with some condominiums and apartments near the water. Lots are typically small, with limited parking, and newer rebuilds have replaced a few older structures. The Sunset Cliffs side and the streets near Point Loma's slopes feature homes with ocean views and bigger lots. Few HOAs exist outside of condo buildings. Prices are well above the county average near the ocean and moderate in the more interior blocks.

What Shapes Value in Ocean Beach

Ocean and sunset views, bluff-top location and the age of the housing stock are the main variables. Older homes may have unpermitted additions, and coastal zone requirements apply to many properties.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

The fishing pier, the off-leash beach at the river mouth, the Sunset Cliffs bluffs and the antique and surf shops of Newport Avenue anchor daily life here.

Our family home near Sunset Cliffs has an unpermitted back unit; how do appraisers deal with it?

The appraiser reports the legal improvements and describes the unit as an unpermitted addition. Value for the lawful part is developed with comparable sales, and the report discloses any effect on marketability or financing.
