Home Appraisals in Jamul

Jamul is a rural enclave in the foothills east of Chula Vista, with rolling hills, oak trees and large residential parcels. It combines a country setting with relatively easy access to the South Bay and East County.

The Jamul Housing Market

Most homes are custom or semi-custom houses on one to ten acres, built from the 1970s onward, plus older ranch homes. Horse properties and detached guest units are common. A few planned communities, such as Rancho Jamul, have private roads and HOA dues. Septic and well systems are widespread. Pricing is above the East County average and reflects acreage and views.

What Shapes Value in Jamul

Views, usable acreage, steep terrain, road access and well production can change value between neighboring parcels. Fire hazard zones are significant, and outbuildings or unpermitted additions should be verified. Large custom homes can be difficult to match with recent sales.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Notable places include Steele Canyon, Lyons Valley, Proctor Valley, Jamul Indian Village and Lake Jennings's nearby East County corridor.

We own a custom home on several acres in Jamul. How are the extra acres and the horse facilities treated?

The appraiser considers usable land area, topography and the quality of barns, arenas and fencing, using sales of similar horse properties where possible. The extra acres add value to the degree that the market pays for them. A separate opinion on the land alone can be provided if needed.
