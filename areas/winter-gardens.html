Home Appraisals in Winter Gardens

Winter Gardens is a residential section of Lakeside near the river's southern bank. The area developed from rural land into a neighborhood of mid-century houses and manufactured housing.

The Winter Gardens Housing Market

Most homes are modest single-family houses from the 1950s to 1970s, alongside a notable number of manufactured homes and mobile home parks. Lots are generally flat and sized for small yards, with some larger parcels. Condos are rare, and HOA presence is limited. Pricing sits in the lower end of the county range, reflecting the age and size of the stock.

What Shapes Value in Winter Gardens

Manufactured homes require attention to foundation status, year and land ownership, which affects both financing and value. Flood zones, aging systems and added rooms are routine review items. Comparable data tends to be narrower, so sale selection depends on matching construction type.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Winter Gardens Boulevard, Maine Avenue, Lakeside Village and the Interstate 8 corridor are the area's reference points.

Does a manufactured home in Winter Gardens get appraised differently from a stick-built house?

Yes. It must be compared with similar manufactured homes, and the appraiser verifies whether it sits on a permanent foundation and if the land is owned or leased. Those details influence both value and lender eligibility. The report documents them clearly.
