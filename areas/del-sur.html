Home Appraisals in Del Sur

Del Sur is a newer planned community in northern San Diego with a village-style layout and homes organized around parks, paseos, and a central commercial node. Because it was built in a short period by a limited group of builders, sales comparisons are usually very focused.

The Del Sur Housing Market

Nearly all homes were built in the 2000s and 2010s, mostly detached two-story houses with a variety of architectural styles, supplemented by townhome and condominium products. Lots are small to mid-sized, and the neighborhoods have names such as Mandolin, Garretson, Skye, and Alverton. HOAs are present throughout, and many parcels pay Mello-Roos special taxes. Prices are in the upper tier, at the higher end of the surrounding area. Because inventory is limited, one or two sales can shape the comparable set.

What Shapes Value in Del Sur

Special-tax amounts and HOA dues vary among the tracts, making tract-level comparison essential. Lot position near parks, open space, or the edges of the community affects view and privacy. Newer homes can include solar leases or other items that need review.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Lake Hodges, Black Mountain Ranch open space, the Del Sur village center, and neighborhood parks and trails are the main local features.

The home I inherited in Del Sur has leased solar panels. Does that change the valuation?

A leased system is not owned by the property and generally contributes little value, although buyers may need to assume the contract. An owned system is treated differently and may add value. The appraiser reviews the lease terms and discusses how the market reacts.
