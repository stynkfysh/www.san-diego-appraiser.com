Home Appraisals in Jacumba Hot Springs

Jacumba Hot Springs is a small desert town on the Mexican border, near the Imperial County line, with a historic hot-springs resort past. It has a very small market that attracts retirees, artists and investors.

The Jacumba Hot Springs Housing Market

Homes are mostly small bungalows and cabins from the early to mid twentieth century, along with manufactured homes and rural parcels on larger lots. Several lots are vacant, and some buildings are in need of repair. Wells and septic or community systems serve different areas. No condominium market exists, and HOAs are rare. Prices are modest by county standards.

What Shapes Value in Jacumba Hot Springs

Condition, flood and drainage patterns, water supply, and the legal status of older structures are important. Border proximity and distance from services can affect marketability. Renovation of historic lots requires careful review of permits.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Local places include the Jacumba Hot Springs spa and resort, Old Highway 80, the Jacumba Valley, the In-Ko-Pah Mountains and Anza-Borrego Desert State Park to the north.

We bought an older Jacumba cabin to restore. Will an appraiser value it as is or after the work?

Either approach is possible. An as-is appraisal reflects the current condition and any needed repairs, while a subject-to-completion value uses the renovation plans and budget. Lenders and estate attorneys often ask for both.
