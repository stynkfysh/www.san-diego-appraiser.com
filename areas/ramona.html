Home Appraisals in Ramona

Ramona sits in an inland valley northeast of Poway and is known for larger lots, horse properties and a rural lifestyle within commuting range of the metro area. Appraisals there rely on a wider comparable search radius than in the coastal cities.

The Ramona Housing Market

The housing mix includes ranch homes and manufactured houses on one to five acres, older farmhouses near Main Street, and the large planned community of San Diego Country Estates. Newer custom homes sit on hillside parcels, and some tracts have HOAs. Condominiums are rare. Many homes use septic systems and private wells. Prices are generally below the central county average, with premiums for acreage and views.

What Shapes Value in Ramona

Septic, well capacity and access roads affect value and lending. Fire hazard severity zones are widespread. Equestrian improvements, outbuildings and usable acreage require careful adjustment, and gross living area is only part of the value picture.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Nearby places include the Ramona Airport, Ramona Grasslands Preserve, Lake Sutherland, Mount Woodson and Santa Maria Valley.

My mother's Ramona property has five acres, a well and a barn. How do you value the land and outbuildings?

Land and improvements are analyzed together using sales of similar acreage with comparable utilities and access. Barns and arenas are given contributory value based on condition, and the well and septic are noted. Where good matches are scarce, the search extends across the Ramona area.
