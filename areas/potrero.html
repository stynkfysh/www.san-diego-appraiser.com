Home Appraisals in Potrero

Potrero is a tiny rural community in the southern backcountry, reached by State Route 94 near the border. Its few homes are on large lots, and valuation work usually involves multi-year sales data.

The Potrero Housing Market

Housing is mainly detached ranch houses, cabins and manufactured homes on parcels of several acres. A handful of larger agricultural properties and horse facilities are present. Wells and septic systems serve nearly every home, and there are no condominium developments. HOAs are uncommon. Prices are low relative to the county, particularly for older or smaller structures.

What Shapes Value in Potrero

Value depends on water reliability, road condition, parcel size and wildfire exposure, and flood or drainage patterns along local creeks can matter. With very few sales, the appraiser relies on neighboring backcountry communities.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Local landmarks include Potrero County Park, the Potrero Valley, Barrett Lake and the surrounding Cleveland National Forest lands.

I have a few acres and a small house in Potrero. Is a standard appraisal report enough for a family transfer?

Generally yes, provided the report clearly identifies the ownership interest and uses the right effective date. If the parcel may be split or the use is unusual, additional analysis can be added. An attorney can specify what is needed for the transfer.
