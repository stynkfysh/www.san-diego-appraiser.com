Home Appraisals in Alpine

Alpine is a foothill community along Interstate 8, with oak woodlands, ranch parcels and a small town center. It combines rural properties with newer subdivisions, and appraisals reflect that variety.

The Alpine Housing Market

The stock includes older ranch houses, custom homes on multi-acre lots, and tract homes in newer areas such as Crown Hills. Horse properties and detached shops appear throughout, and a few condos and townhomes are near the town center. HOAs are limited, though some master-planned tracts have them. Pricing is generally upper-middle for the county, with acreage and view parcels higher.

What Shapes Value in Alpine

Wells, septic systems, steep driveways and fire hazard zones all influence value, as do road type and utility service. Large parcels may include income-producing uses or accessory dwellings that need permit review. Sales are less frequent than in suburban areas, so comparable distance and adjustments need clear rationale.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Viejas Casino, Loveland Reservoir, Alpine Boulevard and the Interstate 8 corridor are the area's main references.

We own five acres in Alpine with a guest cottage; how does the appraiser treat the second dwelling?

The guest cottage is valued as part of the property if it is legal and permitted, with attention to size, condition and utilities. If unpermitted, its contribution is limited or excluded. Comparable multi-structure properties help support the figure.
