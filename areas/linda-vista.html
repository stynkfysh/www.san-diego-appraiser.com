Home Appraisals in Linda Vista

Linda Vista occupies a mesa north of Mission Valley and west of Interstate 805, and was originally built as wartime housing in the 1940s. Appraisers see mainly small, older homes alongside apartments and newer townhomes.

The Linda Vista Housing Market

The core of the neighborhood is made up of modest single-story houses and duplexes from the 1940s and 1950s on regular lots. Apartment complexes line Linda Vista Road and Genesee Avenue, and condominiums and townhome projects have been added in recent years. Some blocks have canyon edges with limited view potential. Many properties are owner-occupied, though investor ownership is common near the University of San Diego. Prices are moderate by county standards, with remodeled homes and ADU properties commanding more.

What Shapes Value in Linda Vista

The age of the housing and the number of unpermitted conversions are the most common appraisal considerations. Lots with legal ADUs or junior ADUs should be compared with similar properties, and nearby apartment density affects the setting.

Local Landmarks and Amenities

Local landmarks include the University of San Diego, Linda Vista Community Park, the Linda Vista Library and the Morena Boulevard corridor.

There is a converted garage at the back of the Linda Vista house we are listing; is it counted in the square footage?

Only if it was legally converted with permits and meets the finished-living-area standards. Otherwise it is described separately and not counted in the gross living area, though it may still add some market value as an extra room.
