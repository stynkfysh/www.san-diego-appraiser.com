How the Final Fee Is Set

The prices above apply to typical homes on typical lots. Very large or high-value properties, acreage, mixed-use parcels, retrospective dates that require extra research, and rush deadlines can raise the fee. You will always see the exact amount in writing before the assignment is scheduled.

Choosing a Report Type

Desktop reports suit situations where everyone involved agrees on the home's condition, such as many trust administrations and family buyouts. Drive-by reports add an exterior observation. Standard reports include an interior walkthrough and are the safest choice when a value may be contested, when the condition is unusual, or when a court is likely to review the work.

Payment

Payment is due when the report is delivered and can be made by card, check or electronic transfer. Attorneys and fiduciaries handling several properties can ask about invoicing.
