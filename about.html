About

About the Appraiser

The person who inspects the home is the person who signs the report.

Since 2004, Brian Ward has appraised residential property in Southern California, completing more than 7,000 assignments. His practice, Brian Ward Appraisal, concentrates on the appraisals that support legal, tax and family decisions rather than mortgage lending: date-of-death and trust valuations, divorce, bankruptcy, tax appeals and litigation.

This site is the San Diego County directory of that practice. It exists so that a trustee in Fallbrook, an attorney in Chula Vista or a homeowner in Santee can see that their community is covered and what an appraisal there will consider. The main practice website is brianward.com, which also covers Riverside County.

Standards

Every report is prepared under the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP) and signed by the appraiser who did the work. Assignments are accepted only when they can be completed competently and without a conflict of interest.
