Insurance Dispute Appraisals in San Diego County

Fire season in the foothills, water losses in older central neighborhoods and coverage questions after a major repair can all leave an owner and an insurer far apart. An independent market-value appraisal gives the dispute a factual footing.

Questions an appraisal can answer

Pre-loss and post-loss market value, the effect of a loss or a stigma on value, and whether coverage limits reflect what the property is worth. Replacement-cost estimating is a separate discipline; the report addresses market value and states its scope plainly.

Clear for adjusters and counsel

Reports are written for adjusters, umpires and attorneys, with photographs and a direct explanation of how the conclusion was reached.

Fees

Insurance assignments are quoted by scope after a short conversation about the claim.
