Pre-Sale Appraisals for San Diego County Sellers

Overpricing costs sellers time and leverage, while underpricing gives away equity. A pre-listing appraisal anchors the asking price to recent closed sales and current conditions in the specific neighborhood, not to a hoped-for figure.

Especially helpful for

Estate and trust sales where a fiduciary must justify the price, for-sale-by-owner listings, homes that have been heavily remodeled, and properties where the seller and agent disagree on strategy.

A head start on the buyer's appraisal

When the price is supported before listing, the risk of a low lender appraisal later in escrow drops. Sellers often share the report with buyers or keep it in reserve for negotiation.
