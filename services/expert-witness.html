Appraisal service

Expert Testimony & Litigation Valuations, San Diego County

Litigation puts an appraisal under a level of scrutiny that ordinary assignments never face. The work has to be careful at the outset and explainable later, often months afterward, to a judge, jury or arbitrator who has never seen the property.

Matters handled

Partition actions among co-owners, partnership and LLC buyouts, construction-defect and diminution-in-value claims, real estate fraud, breach of contract, family-law disputes and contested estate valuations.

Review of another appraiser's work

When the other side has an appraisal, a review can identify unsupported adjustments, weak comparables, misread market trends or a scope that does not fit the question. Review findings can be delivered in writing or as consulting support to counsel.

Fees

Litigation engagements are quoted individually according to scope, deadlines and expected testimony time. Call to discuss the matter before any documents are exchanged.
