Tax Appraisals in San Diego County

Tax agencies expect evidence, not opinions. Whether the issue is an assessed value that looks out of step with the market or a gift of real estate that must be reported at fair market value, the appraisal is the evidence.

Assessment appeals

After a market softening, a purchase at a premium, or a new-construction assessment, a home's assessed value can exceed what it would actually sell for. A valuation as of the relevant lien date, with comparable support organized for the Assessment Appeals Board, is the core of an appeal.

Gift and transfer reporting

Deeding property to children, adding a family member to title, or transferring interests in a family entity can trigger IRS reporting at fair market value. A qualified appraisal supports the figure on the return.

Fees

Tax assignments are quoted by report type and by how far back the effective date reaches.
