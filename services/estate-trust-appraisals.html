Estate & Trust Appraisals for San Diego County Fiduciaries

Trustees and executors carry a legal duty to treat beneficiaries fairly, and real estate is where that duty is most often tested. A credible appraisal gives the fiduciary a defensible value for funding, allocating, selling or distributing a property.

Typical estate assignments

Valuing a residence before it is distributed in kind, setting a price when one beneficiary buys out the others, documenting values for a probate inventory, supporting an accounting to the court, and establishing the date-of-death basis for income-tax purposes.

Several properties, one engagement

Estates frequently hold a primary home plus a rental, a vacant parcel or a condo elsewhere in the county. Multi-property assignments can be scheduled together, with consistent methodology across every report and a combined quote up front.

Built for review

Fiduciary work is read by people who were not in the room: beneficiaries, opposing counsel, a probate referee. The reports are written to be followed step by step, which tends to settle disagreements before they become disputes.
