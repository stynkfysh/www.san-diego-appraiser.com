Immigration Appraisals in San Diego County

Several immigration filings ask a sponsor or applicant to demonstrate assets, and equity in a home is often the most significant one. A certified appraisal turns that equity into documentation an officer can accept.

What the report provides

A current market value supported by comparable sales, a description of the property and its condition, and the appraiser's certification. Attorneys typically pair the appraisal with a mortgage statement to show net equity.

Cross-border families

San Diego County's border communities produce a steady number of these assignments. Reports are written in clear English suitable for submission with the petition.
